# Label defects found by looking (before the fix)

Rendered by `scripts/label-shots.mjs` in the desktop app, hidden (`MAPMAKER_TEST=1`, screenshots through `webContents.capturePage()`), world seed 12345 at 200k cells (seed 777 at 1M), theme atlas unless named. The matrix: flat map in equirectangular, Robinson and orthographic; zoom 1, 2, 4, 8; themes atlas, physical, parchment, political; globe at three rotations; 200k and 1M cells; labels on and off. Images below are in this folder as `before-*.png` (small copies; the full matrix is regenerated by the script). Counts come from the page's DOM boxes (bounding boxes, so rotated path labels overstate overlaps).

Ordered by severity.

1. **River and range labels are unreadable** (`before-02`, `before-09`, `before-11`, `before-13`, `before-15`). The river path is the jagged chain of mesh cells; text laid along it twists, glyphs overlap, runs upside down and mirror-wraps ("Kereth River" reads as noise). Every river label in every shot is affected; ranges never get a label at all at zoom 1.
2. **Ocean labels run over land** (`before-02`: MARIC OCEAN over Gossa, SHOURAN OCEAN across Shenor; `before-03`). The label is centred on the basin's anchor with no idea where the coast is, and the solver may then push it further onto land.
3. **Labels overlap each other** (`before-02`: BROULAN OCEAN, SHENOR and Kereth River; `before-15`: DRONDAR with Nath River, Vana/Both). 4 to 5 overlapping pairs on a 24-label map; the solver has too few legal positions because the candidates are not filtered and path labels cannot move.
4. **Labels leave the map outline** (`before-02`: SHOURAN OCEAN is cut at the Robinson edge; `before-15`: DRONDAR clipped by the right edge). The drawing area is the bounding rectangle, not the projection's outline.
5. **Globe: labels cross the limb and sit in black space** (`before-11`, `before-13`: BROULAN OCEAN and GRETHAN OCEAN lie half outside the sphere; GOSSA hangs over the edge; river text sits on the limb upside down). No fade near the limb, no size change for foreshortening.
6. **Zoom does not work** (`before-05`, zoom 4: 7 labels and no continent, sea or range; zoom 8: one label). Area labels need their anchor inside the view, so a continent that fills the view loses its name; nothing re-anchors to the visible part.
7. **No hierarchy** (all): continents, oceans, islands differ little in size; every label has a heavy white halo that looks like a sticker (continent and island names on land); river labels at 8 px with a halo as thick as the letters.
8. **Too few labels at zoom 1** (`before-02`: 24 labels, no seas, no ranges, 2 islands on a 1000 px world).
9. **Weak contrast in dark water** (`before-02`, physical theme): blue italic ocean names on mid-blue.
10. **Pan and rotation stability** (measured, 200k, Robinson): small pans move 3 of 72 labels at zoom 1 (slid along their path), lose 1 of 21 at zoom 4; rotating the orthographic view by 1, 3 and 6 degrees makes 2 labels appear or vanish and jumps 1 (69 labels). Not dramatic; the new code must not regress it.

Not defects (checked): the names are already pronounceable and deterministic (`packages/carto/src/names.ts`, a syllable grammar marked as a placeholder for Phase 18), there are no "Landmass 7" codes in any shot.
